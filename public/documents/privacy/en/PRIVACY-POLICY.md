# Privacy Policy

karasu256.com ("we", "us", or "the Site") sets out this Privacy Policy (the "Policy") to explain how we handle the personal information of users of the services we provide (the "Service").

## Article 1 (Information We Collect)

To provide the Service, we may collect the following information.

- Account information: name (display name), email address, profile image, and email verification status
- Authentication information: passwords, passkey public keys and authenticator details, and linked-account information from external services (such as Google, GitHub, and Microsoft)
- Session information: tokens used to keep you signed in, expiry times, IP address, and browser/device information (User-Agent)
- Settings you create or authorize in the Service, such as API keys, OAuth clients, and consent records
- Files, such as images, that you upload to the Service
- Information you provide when contacting us

## Article 2 (Purposes of Use)

We use the information we collect for the following purposes.

- To provide the Service, verify identity, authenticate users, and prevent unauthorized access
- To manage accounts and sessions, including switching between multiple accounts
- To send verification codes, password reset messages, and other notifications by email
- To maintain and improve the Service, respond to incidents, and ensure security
- To respond to violations of the Terms of Service
- To respond to inquiries from users

## Article 3 (Protection and Encryption of Information)

We take necessary and appropriate security measures to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of the information we hold. In particular, highly sensitive information is stored encrypted or hashed, as shown in the following examples.

- Passwords: stored as a hash so the original cannot be recovered. We do not retain your password itself.
- Access tokens, refresh tokens, and ID tokens from linked external services: stored encrypted in the database.
- Private keys used for signing: stored encrypted.
- Email verification codes (one-time codes) and approval tokens: stored hashed; expired values can no longer be used.
- OAuth client secrets: shown only once at issuance and stored hashed.
- Processing details held temporarily for internal jobs (e.g. batch context): held encrypted.
- Session (cookie) identifiers: managed as hashed values in the cache.

Communications are carried over encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, we cannot completely guarantee the security of communications or storage over the Internet.

## Article 4 (Disclosure to Third Parties)

We do not provide personal information to third parties without the user's prior consent, except in the following cases.

- When required by law
- When necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property and it is difficult to obtain the user's consent
- When it is necessary to cooperate with a national or local government body, or a party entrusted by one, in carrying out duties prescribed by law

## Article 5 (Outsourcing and Use of External Services)

To provide the Service, we may use external services for authentication, databases, email delivery, and file delivery. We allow these providers to handle only the information necessary within the scope of the purposes of use, and we strive to ensure it is properly managed.

When you sign in through an external service (such as Google, GitHub, or Microsoft), the information provided by that service is handled in accordance with this Policy.

## Article 6 (Use of Cookies)

We use cookies to keep you signed in and to ensure security. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the Service may then become unavailable.

## Article 7 (Disclosure, Correction, and Deletion)

Users may request disclosure, correction, or deletion of their personal information held by us. Some account information can be changed or deleted from the account management screen. When you make a request, we will verify your identity and respond within a reasonable scope.

## Article 8 (Retention Period)

We retain user information for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes of use. When an account is deleted, we delete related information, except information we are required by law to retain.

## Article 9 (Changes to This Policy)

We may change this Policy as necessary. The revised Policy takes effect when it is posted on the Site.

## Article 10 (Contact)

For inquiries about this Policy, please contact the point of contact we designate separately.
